# 📊 Tax Routines & Workflow Management Hub

A comprehensive collection of spreadsheets, workflow guides, and explanatory documentation designed to streamline tax routines, data cross-referencing, and fiscal compliance.

---

## 🚀 Overview

* **Tax Calculation & Validation:** Managing routine procedures for companies under *Simples Nacional* and *Lucro Presumido* tax regimes, including federal and municipal tax computations.
* **Excel Automation & Workflows:** Utilizing advanced spreadsheets for reconciliations, XML invoice analysis, and report validation from systems like *Sistema Domínio* and government portals (*e-CAC, DCTFWeb, EFD, REINF*).
* **Task Organization:** Structuring schedules and workflows to ensure legal compliance and deadline adherence without errors.

---

## 📂 Featured Guides & Spreadsheets

### 1. Municipal Invoice Cancellation Guide
* **Description:** Explanatory spreadsheet (with up-to-date notes) on municipal invoice cancellation procedures across different city hall portals. 
* **Purpose:** Serves as a practical guide for professionals who need to understand, navigate, and execute invoice cancellations safely across various municipal systems.

### 2. General Tax Activities Tracker
* **Description:** General tax and workflow tracker for task description and organization.
* **Purpose:** Optimizes daily operational tasks, document organization, and fiscal routines.
